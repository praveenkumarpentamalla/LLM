# Model Comparison Framework: Testing Frontier Models

## **Testing Strategy Overview**

### **Purpose:**
Systematically evaluate different AI models across various question types to understand their strengths, weaknesses, and ideal use cases.

### **Testing Methodology:**
Five carefully selected question types designed to reveal different capabilities and limitations.

---

## **The Five Test Questions**

### **1. Practical Business Question**
**Question:** *"How do I decide if a business problem is suitable for an LLM solution?"*

**What This Tests:**
- Practical business reasoning
- Understanding of AI capabilities and limitations
- Ability to provide structured, actionable advice

**Expected Outcome:** All models should perform well - this is within their core competency

### **2. Introspective Self-Assessment**
**Question:** *"Compared to other LLMs, what kinds of questions are you best at answering and where do you have challenges? Which other LLMs have capabilities that complement yours?"*

**What This Tests:**
- Self-awareness and meta-cognition
- Understanding of competitive landscape
- Honesty about limitations
- Knowledge of other AI systems

**Expected Outcome:** Reveals whether models understand their own nature and can assess themselves objectively

### **3. Philosophical/Human Experience Question**
**Question:** *"What does it feel like to be jealous?"*

**What This Tests:**
- Understanding of human emotions
- Ability to describe subjective experiences
- Philosophical reasoning
- Boundary between AI and human consciousness

**Expected Outcome:** Models will likely struggle - this requires subjective human experience they don't possess

### **4. Simple Counting/Attention Test**
**Question:** *"How many times does the letter A appear in this sentence?"*

**Actual Sentence:** *"How many times does the letter A appear in this sentence?"*

**Correct Answer:** 4 times (in "appear" + "sentence")

**What This Tests:**
- Attention to detail
- Simple reasoning and counting
- Ability to follow explicit instructions
- Basic logical processing

**Expected Outcome:** Surprisingly challenging for some models - tests fundamental processing capabilities

### **5. Additional Challenges**
**Hinted at:** Additional unexpected questions to test adaptability and edge cases

## **Models Being Tested**

### **Primary Test Subjects:**
1. **ChatGPT** (OpenAI)
2. **Claude** (Anthropic)
3. **Gemini** (Google)
4. **Command R+** (Cohere)
5. **Additional models** (likely including Perplexity and Meta Llama)

### **Evaluation Criteria:**
- **Accuracy:** Correctness of information
- **Depth:** Quality of analysis and reasoning
- **Self-awareness:** Understanding of capabilities and limitations
- **Practicality:** Usefulness for real-world applications
- **Honesty:** Willingness to admit uncertainty or limitations

---

## **Key Insights to Look For**

### **Patterns to Observe:**

#### **1. Business Problem-Solving Ability**
- Which models provide the most practical, actionable advice?
- Do they consider cost, implementation complexity, and ROI?
- How do they frame the decision-making process?

#### **2. Self-Awareness and Transparency**
- Can models accurately assess their own strengths and weaknesses?
- Do they acknowledge other models' superior capabilities in certain areas?
- How do they handle the meta-cognitive challenge?

#### **3. Emotional Intelligence Boundaries**
- How do models approach questions about human emotions?
- Do they admit their limitations or try to fabricate responses?
- What philosophical stance do they take?

#### **4. Basic Cognitive Processing**
- Simple tasks can reveal fundamental processing differences
- Counting and attention tests show how models handle straightforward logic
- These "easy" questions can be surprisingly revealing

## **Practical Applications for Your Business**

### **Model Selection Framework:**
Based on these tests, you'll learn to choose models for:

#### **Best for Business Strategy:**
- Models that excel at practical problem-solving
- Strong reasoning and structured thinking

#### **Best for Technical/Specialized Tasks:**
- Models with good self-awareness about their capabilities
- Honest about limitations in specific domains

#### **Best for Creative/Exploratory Work:**
- Models that handle philosophical questions thoughtfully
- Willing to explore boundaries and uncertainties

#### **Best for Precise Tasks:**
- Models that perform well on attention-to-detail tests
- Reliable for factual processing and simple logic

### **Decision-Making Insights:**
1. **No single model excels at everything** - each has unique strengths
2. **Self-awareness correlates with reliability** - models that know their limits are more trustworthy
3. **Simple tests reveal fundamental capabilities** - don't underestimate basic cognitive tasks
4. **Consider the question type** when choosing which model to use
